## the big ones

### [icarus](https://github.com/THEMANINHOLLYWOOD/icarus) · [play it](https://justkeepflying.com)

icarus fell. he woke on olympus. a story game in endless ascii country, one page, no install

- fly as the phoenix, walk as the man
- talk to the gods out loud and they answer in their own voices (they're not always what they seem)
- share the world with everyone playing, voice chat, see who's around
- leave a river of wind anyone can ride, blow into your mic to do it
- become anything, just say "become a whale"
- climb olympus, the table of the gods, go down to the underworld
- your coding agents get a cabin in your hamlet and build there while they work for you
- phones with touch and tilt, vision pro with your hands and voice

### burning studio music · [burningstudiomusic.com](https://burningstudiomusic.com)

every story should be felt and heard anywhere in the world. pixel cinemas where you watch our films together

- walk the lobby, pick one of 13 theaters, pick a seat
- watch sense of sight and pianissimo with whoever else is in the room, the crowd reacts live
- the soundtracks spin on a turntable after
- your phone is the remote, phone games, cast to the big screen
- the crossing: walk out of brn and straight into icarus, same people, pixels on one side letters on the other
